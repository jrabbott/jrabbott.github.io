### Competency breakdown

- **Leadership**: Sets clear objectives, tracks progress, and maintains high quality standards.
- **Communication**: Speaks concisely and presents clear, logical arguments.
- **Initiative**: Takes full responsibility for results and pursues goals with high determination.
- **Analysis**: Makes practical decisions based on facts, data, and risk evaluation.
- **Planning**: Builds workable plans, sets priorities, and delegates clearly.
- **Interpersonal**: Maintains professional relationships and focuses on business goals.
