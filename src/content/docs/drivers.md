:::tip[Environment constraints]
Maintain high performance through clear ownership, measurable targets, and direct authority.
:::

### Motivators

- **Managing results**: Setting and achieving measurable targets.
- **Precision work**: Building domain expertise and delivering accurate work.
- **Career growth**: Having clear advancement pathways and recognition.

### Work guidelines

- **Clear ownership**: Ensure single-point accountability for projects.
- **Focused time**: Allocate uninterrupted time for complex work.
- **Purposeful tasks**: Focus energy on high-impact business goals.
