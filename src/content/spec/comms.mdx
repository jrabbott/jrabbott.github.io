### Communication guidelines

- **Be concise**: State the main point quickly and clearly.
- **Use fact-based feedback**: Give clear feedback based on facts and measurable outcomes.
- **Keep meetings focused**: Use clear agendas for meetings and focus on active tasks.
- **Recognise results**: Value direct feedback for strong contributions and fast work.
