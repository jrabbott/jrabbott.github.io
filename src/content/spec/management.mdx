### Operating parameters

- **Provide autonomy**: Trust independent capability and use brief progress reviews.
- **Use logic**: Present plans with business facts, logic, and expert data.
- **Give ownership**: Assign clear authority and ownership for project domains.
- **Align directly**: Discuss changes early using clear facts and clear logic.
